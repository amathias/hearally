Who can use Hearally

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • You create your own account and hold it. Nobody can sign up on your behalf, and you may not create an account for someone else.
  • You are the account holder: you own your data and you set the rules for who hears what.

What Hearally is, and is not

Hearally passes along things that were mentioned out loud near your Plaud recorder to the people you choose. It reports what was said. It does not detect what happened.

  • Hearally is not for emergencies. If someone needs help now, call 911.
  • Hearally is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor any health condition.
  • It reports things that were said out loud. It does not detect falls, accidents or illness.
  • It will miss things, and it will sometimes pass along something that turns out to be nothing.
  • Nobody is standing by. Messages go to the people you choose, and there is no promise that anyone sees or acts on one.
  • Care decisions are yours.

Your responsibilities

  • Record lawfully. Only record conversations you have the legal right to record. Some places, including several US states, require everyone in a conversation, phone calls included, to agree to being recorded. Where the law requires that, get agreement first. You are responsible for what you record and upload, not Hearally.
  • Use Hearally for yourself, not to watch over another adult without their knowledge and agreement.
  • Keep your sign-in to yourself, and tell us if you think someone else has used it.
  • Do not try to break, overload or get around Hearally’s security, or use it to send messages to people who have not agreed to receive them.
