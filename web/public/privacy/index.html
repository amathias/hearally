Privacy

Last updated 9 October 2026.

Hearally listens to part of your life so it can tell the people you choose about the things you would want them to know. That only works if you can trust exactly what happens to what it hears. This page says so plainly.

The short version

  • You decide who hears what. Nobody else receives anything.
  • Full transcripts are never kept: each is deleted as soon as it has been read. What we keep is a short summary of each thing worth passing on, or held back for you alone.
  • Account numbers are silenced in the audio itself, and the unredacted original is deleted.
  • We do not sell your data, show you ads, or use your recordings to train AI models, and neither do the companies that process them for us.
  • Hearally is for people 18 or older.

Only record what you have the right to record

You may only upload recordings you have the legal right to make. The law on recording differs from place to place. Some places, including several US states, require everyone taking part in a conversation, phone calls included, to agree to being recorded. Where the law requires that, you must get that agreement before you record.

You confirm this in your dashboard before any recording comes in, and you are responsible for what you record and upload. Hearally is not able to check whether a recording was made lawfully, and does not do so.

What we collect

Your Plaud connection
If you sign in to Plaud from Hearally, the sign-in Plaud gives us, kept apart from everything else and used only to fetch new recordings.
Consent records

Your allies
The name, email address and relationship of each person you add, what you have chosen to let each one hear, what was sent to them, and the quiet hours, daily-list time and time zone each ally sets for themselves. If an ally chooses phone calls, the mobile number they confirmed, and a record of each call.

How recordings reach us

  • Plaud sign-in. You sign in to Plaud once, on Plaud’s own page. About every 15 minutes, Hearally fetches the audio of recordings made after you connected. Plaud’s approval page lists more than that, including transcripts and summaries, because Plaud offers only one level of access. Hearally does not fetch them. You can disconnect at any time from your dashboard.
  • The Hearally collector. A small program on your computer sends recordings you save to one folder.

How your recordings are processed

  1. Your recording is stored, encrypted, at Amazon Web Services in the United States.
  2. It is transcribed by NVIDIA’s open Parakeet model, running on servers we operate on Nebius AI Cloud.
  3. Account numbers and similar details are found by their timing in the transcript and silenced in the audio. The unredacted original is then deleted. If processing fails, a safeguard deletes it automatically, which can take up to about two days.
  4. To decide what is worth passing on, the transcript, with long numbers masked, is sent to NVIDIA’s open Nemotron model, hosted by Nebius. We use Nebius with zero data retention, which means Nebius does not store what we send once it has answered. Nebius states that it does not use customer content to train any AI model.
  5. What comes back is a short summary, such as “a new appointment on Thursday”. That summary is what we keep. The transcript itself is deleted as soon as it has been read.

Before anything is passed on, our dignity filter holds back private moments, such as venting about family or a remark about your body, and anything that mentions a topic you have named. The one exception: a caller pressing for money or details, or a request for help, is always passed on. Their short summaries are listed for you alone; no ally sees one unless you choose to send it. A film or programme playing in the room is ignored and not kept at all.

Who can see it

  • You can see everything about your account in your dashboard.
  • Your allies see only what you allowed each of them to see, by email and through a private link that expires.
  • Companies that run Hearally for us process data only to provide the service: Amazon Web Services (hosting, storage, email, and the text that confirms an ally's number), Nebius (the AI models), CALL-E (the automated phone calls an ally chooses), and Google Analytics (which pages of this website are visited). None of them may use it for their own purposes.

We do not sell data, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything other than running Hearally for you.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits to the pages of this website, so we can see which pages help. It records which page was opened and the page before it, as a web address without anything after a "?" or "#"; the type of browser and device; and a rough location from the internet address. It sets cookies in your browser for this. It never receives anything from your recordings, your summaries, your allies, your email address or your allies' links, and it is not linked to your account. Google signals and advertising features are off.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Plaud, or any computer, from your dashboard at any time. Nothing new arrives after that.
  • Ask us to delete your summaries, or to close your account, by email to the address below. Your recordings, summaries and connections are then deleted, apart from records we are required by law to keep. Doing this from the dashboard is being built.
  • Change what any ally can hear, or remove an ally, at any time. Allies can stop receiving messages at any time from their own page.
